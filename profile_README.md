### Hi, I'm Mia 👋

I'm a Chicago-based data analyst with an MA in Computational Social Science from the University of Chicago. Before grad school I spent a year at Centene running a production Snowflake database and building Power BI reporting for healthcare operations.

I like building reliable data pipelines and using them to answer questions about how people experience the places they live.

#### Featured work

- **[Fear vs. Reality: The Crime Perception Gap in Chicago](https://github.com/mdsowder/Sowder_Thesis_2026)** — MA thesis. Python pipeline combining 46,528 CPD incidents with 16 Chicago Health Atlas indicators across 77 community areas: factor analysis (implemented from scratch in NumPy), k-means and HDBSCAN clustering, and spatial statistics (Global and Local Moran's I).
- **[Encoding Emotion in Music](https://msowder.substack.com/p/encoding-emotion-in-music-a-multimodal)** — team project benchmarking emotion classification on ~83,000 tracks, with a serverless AWS lyrics pipeline (Lambda, SQS, S3, DynamoDB) and Dask that cut runtime by over 90%. [Code](https://github.com/macs30113-s25/music-emotion-benchmark-AWS) · [write-up](https://msowder.substack.com/p/encoding-emotion-in-music-a-multimodal)
- **[Pandemic Anxiety on Reddit](https://github.com/macs30122-winter25/final-project-raam)** — team project scraping r/Nursing, r/Teachers, and r/HealthAnxiety into a SQLite database and tracking anxiety over time with VADER and LIWC against CDC and WHO survey data.
- **[Normal Clay Co.](https://mdsowder.github.io/normalclayco/)** — a small-shop website I designed and built for my sister's ceramics business in a UX/UI design course: data-driven product pages, a persistent basket, and a checkout flow in vanilla HTML, CSS, and JavaScript. [Code](https://github.com/mdsowder/normalclayco)
